Simulation Overview

You play the role of regional pricing manager for a multiplex chain in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru. Each month you make pricing and screen allocation decisions. Competitors react to your pricing decisions in future months.

Business context

You set weekday and weekend ticket prices for each city. Demand responds to your prices, the current season, and competitive positioning. Your screen allocation scales demand proportionally to average screens.

The objective is to maximise cumulative profit across all months while anticipating competitor reactions.

Cities & screens

Mumbai (20 avg), Delhi (15 avg), Bengaluru (14 avg). You choose screens each month.

Your decisions

Set weekday/weekend prices and screens per city, based on season and competitor prices.

Competitor reactions

Competitors adjust prices next month based on your pricing gap, with random variation.
