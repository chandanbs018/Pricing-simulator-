Simulation Overview

You are regional pricing manager for a multiplex chain in Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru. Each month you set ticket prices and screen allocation. Competitors react to your decisions in subsequent months.

Demand Model

Logit model: your share = EXP(λ·price) / (EXP(yours) + EXP(comp) + μ). μ = muBase × season.

Cities

Mumbai (avg 20 screens), Delhi (avg 15), Bengaluru (avg 14). Allocate screens each month.

Competitor Reaction

Competitor adjusts prices next month based on your pricing gap, with random variation (0.5–1.0×).

Formula Flow

EXP(your price) → Logit share → × Base demand = Raw demand → × (student screens / avg screens) = Capacity adj → × Segment factor = Segment adj → × Season multiplier = Final demand / day
