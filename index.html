Multiplex pricing challenge

You are regional pricing manager for three cities. Each month, set weekday/weekend prices and screen allocations. Demand is driven by logit choice between your cinema, competitor, and outside option. Fixed costs scale with screens used. Objective: maximise cumulative profit.
