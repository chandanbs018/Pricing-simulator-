Round 1 of 6

Welcome

You are the regional pricing manager for a multiplex cinema chain operating in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru. Your task is to set ticket prices month by month and manage profit under capacity constraints and competitive pressure.

In each round (one month), you choose weekday and weekend ticket prices for each city. Demand responds to your prices, the current season, and competitor positioning. Your objective is cumulative profit, not just revenue.

This prototype uses a hard-coded version of the Excel demand and financial model to compute realistic results for the current round.[file:1]

Cities

Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru, each with fixed screen capacity, seats per screen, and shows per day as in the Excel model.[file:1]

Your decisions

Set a weekday and weekend ticket price for each city, based on the limited information you receive about season, competition, and capacity tightness.

Outcomes

The simulator computes demand, attendance, revenue, cost, and profit using the same parameters as the spreadsheet (base demand, elasticities, season multipliers, capacities, and costs).[file:1]
