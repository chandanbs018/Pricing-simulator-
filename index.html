Simulation Overview

You are a regional pricing manager for a multiplex chain. Each month = one round.

Context

Set weekday/weekend prices per city. Demand depends on price, season, competitor bands, capacity. Maximise cumulative profit.

Cities & capacity

Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru: fixed screens & seats.

Decisions

One weekday + weekend price per city each month.

Outcome

Profit = revenue – variable – fixed costs.
