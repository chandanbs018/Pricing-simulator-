Simulation Overview

You play the role of regional pricing manager for a multiplex chain in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru. Each round is one month of operations.

Multi-Round Structure

Instructors can configure all rounds in advance with different scenarios, seasons, and market conditions. Students then progress through each round sequentially.

Each round builds on previous learning, with increasing complexity and varying market conditions.

Round Configuration

Instructors set up all rounds in advance with different seasons, scenarios, and competitor settings.

Student Progression

Students see each round's specific context and make pricing decisions accordingly.

Cumulative Learning

Results from all rounds are saved and can be analyzed to understand pricing strategy impact over time.
