Strategic Pricing Lab: Full Economics Model

You are the regional pricing manager for a multiplex chain in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru. Each round represents one month of operations with complete P&L visibility.

📊 What You'll Analyze

Volume Metrics

• Base demand vs. actual attendance

• Lost demand / capacity constraints

• Utilization rates

Financial Metrics

• Revenue by segment (weekday/weekend)

• Variable & fixed cost breakdown

• Contribution margin & profit

Strategic KPIs

• Price vs. competitor positioning

• Seasonality impact

• Elasticity effects

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand price elasticity of demand
  • Analyze trade-off between yield and utilization
  • Manage capacity constraints
  • Optimize across market segments
  • Respond to seasonal demand shifts

📈 Economic Model

Demand: Q = Base × Season × (P/Pcomp)^elasticity

Elasticity: Mumbai: -1.4, Delhi: -1.3, Bengaluru: -1.5

Capacity: Fixed seats per month per city

Costs: Variable (₹28-35/ticket) + Fixed daily
