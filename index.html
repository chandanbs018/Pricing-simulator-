Simulation Overview

Excel‑aligned pricing simulation for multiplex chains in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru.

Model Structure Excel Exact

Demand calculation (per the Excel file):

  1. Exp of Your Price = EXP(λ × Your Price)
  2. Exp of Competition Price = EXP(λ × Comp Price)
  3. Sum of Exp = Exp(Your) + Exp(Comp) + Mu
  4. Mu = 40 × Segment Adjustment Factor
  5. Your Demand = (Exp(Your) / Sum of Exp) × Base Demand
  6. Capacity Adj Demand = Your Demand × (Screens Allocated / Avg Screens)
  7. Movie Segment Adj = Segment Factor × Capacity Adj Demand
  8. Season Adj = Season Multiplier × Movie Segment Adj

This exactly matches the Excel calculation flow.

Key Parameters

λ (lambda) = price sensitivity
Mu = 40 × segment factor
Segment factors: Blockbuster 1.0375, Mainstream 1.01, Niche 0.95

Your Decisions

Set weekday/weekend prices + screens allocated each month. Competitor prices adjust based on your price changes.

Outputs

Demand, attendance, lost sales, revenue, costs, and profit by city and month.
