Simulation Overview

You play the role of regional pricing manager for a multiplex chain in Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru. Each month you make pricing and screen allocation decisions.

Business context v5.0 Monthly

You set weekday and weekend ticket prices for each city and decide how many screens to allocate. Demand is calculated using a logit share model based on your price vs competitor price, then adjusted by movie segment, season, and scenario multipliers. Screens only affect your supply capacity — they do not reduce underlying demand.

The objective is to maximise cumulative profit across all months.

Cities and capacity

Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru each have fixed screens, seats per screen, and shows per day. You choose how many screens to use each month.

Your decisions

For every month you choose weekday and weekend prices per city, plus screens allocated, based on season and competitor prices.

How outcomes are calculated

Logit share model: exp(λ·P_your) / (exp(λ·P_your) + exp(λ·P_comp)) × total market demand. Attendance is then limited by screen capacity.
